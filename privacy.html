Privacy
policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

This site collects nothing about you, runs no analytics, and sets no tracking cookies.

That is the whole policy in one sentence. Everything below is the detail behind it, written plainly rather than in legal boilerplate.

What is stored on your device

The view counter is recorded once per browsing session, using a flag in session storage that is discarded when you close the tab. One further small value is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, is not sent anywhere, and cannot identify you:

  • Your theme choice — whether you switched the page to light or dark.

Clearing your browser data removes both. Nothing else is stored, and no cookies are set by this site.

What loads from other companies

Two files are fetched from external services to make the page work. Both can see your IP address, as any web request does:

  • Google Fonts — the typefaces used throughout. Covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — the 3D library that runs the interactive piece at the bottom of the page. Covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) — only if you press the “Chameleon” button in the interactive piece, which fetches one extra model-loading script. Press nothing and it is never contacted.
  • Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) — the view counter in the footer. It increases a single number against a key for this site. It is sent no information about you beyond the request itself, and it sets no cookies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The support prompt and video

There is no advertising on this site. No ad network, no ad script, no third-party ad cookies — none of it.

A short prompt appears when you arrive, offering a five-second video. The video file is served from this site itself, not from YouTube, Vimeo or any ad network, so watching it tells nobody anything about you and sets no cookies.

Nothing is recorded about whether you watch it or decline, which is why the prompt appears on each visit rather than remembering your answer.

The contact form

The form works, and what you type in it is sent to me. When you press send, your name, email address, chosen topic and message are posted to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a relay service that forwards the message to my inbox. Nothing is stored on this site — it has no database and no server of its own.

That means your message passes through a third party in transit. FormSubmit handles it under its own privacy policy. Your email address is used to reply to you and for nothing else: no list, no newsletter, no sharing.

There is one hidden field in the form that you will never see. It exists to catch automated spam — real visitors leave it empty, and anything that fills it in is discarded without being sent.

If you would rather not use the form, the contact section has an email address you can write to directly.

Your rights

Browsing this site collects nothing about you, so there is nothing held to access, correct, export or delete — the only data connected to your visit lives in your own browser, and clearing your site data removes it.

If you have sent a message through the form, that message and your email address sit in my inbox. Ask me to delete it and I will.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
