Plate M — Studio

The
studio

Est. 2017

Nine years in, I still judge a brand the same way: print it, hold it, and see whether it holds up under a desk lamp. Screens flatter everything. Paper doesn't.

I work as a one-person studio with a rotating bench of collaborators — a photographer in Lisbon, a type designer in Rotterdam, a risograph shop twenty minutes from my desk. Small enough that you talk to the person doing the work; connected enough that a project can grow a custom typeface or a six-colour print run when it needs one.

The work runs from full identity systems through to the unglamorous parts nobody photographs: spacing tables, ink-limit charts, a 40-page guideline that a junior at your agency can actually follow. That's the part that decides whether an identity lasts three years or three months.

Currently taking two projects per quarter so each one gets a real press check.
